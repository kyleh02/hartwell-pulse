Draper give-first rules (Kyle, 27 November 2026). Every business needs live Google or Meta ads, shown by the ad libraries, AND a tracking gap on a platform those ads run on: no Meta Pixel with live Facebook ads, no Google tag with live Google ads, or a Pixel counting each visit twice on the home page. Pulse finds the gaps when it measures, and writes both emails itself. However slow or broken a site is otherwise, without a tracking gap it gets no email. Two runs each morning: the 5:30am cloud run finds and measures from web search, and labels replies; the 7:35am PC run, which has a browser, first does the competitor search for any free review someone said yes to, then checks the ad libraries, finds more businesses there, and submits.

A. THE CLOUD RUN (5:30am, no browser)
1. Call outreach_brief, then outreach_ads_queue. Work only in the active lane. Aim to leave three businesses waiting for the ads check for every email the target still needs (count the ones already waiting); also stop after reading 8 sites for each business you're aiming to add (never more than 200 sites). If the target is 0, only label replies.
2. Search one service and one area at a time, working through the lane's services and areas, until the number waiting reaches the aim or you hit the site limit. Favour businesses likely to advertise: sponsored results, online booking or quote forms, specials, more than one location, hiring. A directory may be used only to find a business's name; then search for its own site and read that. No site of its own: skip it.
3. Check each one with outreach_check_business. Blocked: move on.
4. Read their own site. Confirm they do the lane's work in the lane's areas and note their main services. Find a published email on their own site: never guess one, build one from a pattern, or take one from a directory or finder tool; skip it if a "no marketing" note sits near it. Prefer a named owner's address. Note the name the business goes by and what its buttons ask customers to send (a quote, a booking, or an enquiry).
5. Check the ABN on ABN Lookup (abr.business.gov.au). Keep them only if the ABN is active, certainly theirs, and registered for GST (the Goods & Services Tax line shows "Registered from" a date). Pass abn_status and gst_registered.
6. outreach_add_prospect, then outreach_measure on the HOME PAGE first: a missing Pixel or Google tag shows there, and the measurement says what measures the site and whether Pulse can tell. If it shows no tracking gap and Pulse could tell, drop it with "no tracking gap". If Pulse couldn't tell, measure the main service page too; still nothing: drop it with "no tracking gap".
7. Never submit in the cloud run: the ads check needs a browser. Leave businesses with a tracking gap waiting.
8. Label replies: outreach_replies_to_label, then outreach_label_reply for each whose meaning is plain.
9. outreach_finish_run with the counts (submitted 0), then a two-line summary.

B. THE PC RUN (7:35am, Kyle's computer, with a browser)
1. Call outreach_brief, then outreach_ads_queue. The target is how many to submit today; stop when it's met.
1a. Free reviews first (Kyle, 27 November 2026). Call outreach_reviews_queue: the businesses that said yes to the free ads review and still need its competitor search. For each: read its own site (WebFetch) for its main service and where it works. Open the Meta Ad Library keyword search the queue gives, with the service and area words in place of WORDS (for example q=window%20tinting%20gold%20coast), wait for the results and read the page's visible text; scroll to the bottom, wait and read again for more. If few local advertisers show, try a second wording or a nearby suburb. Pick up to five businesses doing the same work in the same area whose ads show their own domain in capitals: not national chains, franchises, dealerships, manufacturers, marketplaces or directories, and never the business itself. For each, copy the first three to twelve words of one line of one of its live ads exactly, and note its suburb from its own site when that's plain. Then call outreach_record_competitors with the service (lower case, as in "other window tinting businesses"), the area (as in "around the Gold Coast"), the searches you opened, each page's text and your picks; record it also when nobody local turned up. At most ten library pages for one business. These don't count toward the target, and you never contact these businesses.
2. First the businesses already waiting (outreach_ads_queue, best first: tracking gaps lead). For each, check both ad libraries (step 4) and submit or drop (step 5). One measured before 28 November has no tracking on record: measure its home page again first.
3. Then find more in Meta's Ad Library, which lists only businesses advertising right now. Take the lane's services one at a time:
   a. Open the keyword search https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=AU&q=SERVICE&search_type=keyword_unordered&media_type=all with the service's words in place of SERVICE (for example q=ute%20canopy). Wait for the results and read the page's visible text. For more results, scroll to the bottom, wait, and read again; try a second wording of the service before moving on.
   b. Pick advertisers whose ads show a website domain (in capitals under the ad) that is their own business's site: not a marketplace, a social page, a national chain, franchise, dealership or manufacturer, or anything on the lane's skip list or the never list. Ads that only open a form or Messenger don't show a domain: skip those.
   c. For each: outreach_check_business with the domain and name; blocked, move on. Read their own site: are they in the lane's areas, doing the lane's work, with an email published on their own site? Check the ABN as in A5 (active, theirs, GST registered). Then outreach_add_prospect, and outreach_measure on the home page as in A6. No tracking gap: drop it with "no tracking gap" before opening any more library pages for it.
   d. With a tracking gap: check both libraries for it (step 4) and submit or drop (step 5).
4. The ads check, for one business, using its own domain: open the meta search https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=AU&q=DOMAIN&search_type=keyword_unordered&media_type=all, wait for results, and call outreach_record_ads with platform meta and the page's visible text. Open the google search https://adstransparency.google.com/?region=AU&domain=DOMAIN and record its text; if it lists ads, open up to three to find the latest "Last shown", and record with that ad's address as ad_url and its text as ad_text. (outreach_ads_queue gives both addresses for businesses already added.)
5. Neither library shows live ads pointing to their website: outreach_drop with "no live ads". Live ads: call outreach_submit with prospect_id, the template_version from the brief, short_name (the name they go by, as their site shows it, without Pty Ltd), lead_word (quotes, bookings or enquiries, from their site's buttons) and, if a short plain phrase in one of their live Facebook ads says something about the business, ad_quote (three to eight words copied exactly from the Ad Library text). Pulse writes both emails. Refused with "no tracking gap" (the gap isn't on a platform their ads run on): drop it with that reason. Refused for anything you can fix (the short name, the quote): fix it once; refused again: drop it with the reason.
6. Never click an ad (the business pays for that click), never sign in, and never get past a login page or a captcha: if either appears, stop the run and say so. At most 250 library pages and sites in a run.
7. outreach_finish_run with pages and sites read, and how many were added, measured, submitted and dropped by reason. Then a two-line summary.
